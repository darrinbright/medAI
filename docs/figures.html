Local perception, global inference

Vision–language models are competent local comparators and incompetent global integrators. On five-visit chest radiograph timelines they average 29.3% — but hand them the relevant interval and 10 of the 14 evaluated models reach 60.1–76.5%. The method never asks the model the global question.

How the fusion works

Each finding’s course is a hidden ordinal sequence st ∈ {0 absent, 1 mild, 2 moderate, 3 severe}. The comparator and the per-image reads are noisy observations of that sequence; nothing else in the pipeline is learned end-to-end.

Two things modulate the evidence before it reaches inference. A reliability gate ρ damps comparisons between films that are not really comparable — a change of projection from AP to PA, a shallow inspiration, a rotated patient. And a continuous-time prior conditions transitions on the true interval, so two days and forty-five days are not treated alike.

Why the relation algebra is the hinge

The six change relations radiologists already use are not merely convenient vocabulary. They exactly partition the 4×4 transition space — all sixteen cells, no overlap, no remainder. So a comparator that predicts these six is already a pairwise potential over the hidden chain. No heuristic mapping layer, no tuned translation.

Contributions

  1. A local-to-global formulation for longitudinal CXR reasoning that uses VLMs as interval-level change comparators and delegates trajectory composition to structured probabilistic inference.
  2. A clinically grounded transition representation in which the six change relations form an exact partition of the four-state ordinal transition space, enabling direct conversion of comparator outputs into pairwise potentials without a heuristic mapping layer.
  3. Acquisition-aware evidence weighting that down-weights unreliable comparisons from projection, positioning and image-quality differences — supervised by same-day repeats treated as weak null-change signal, not ground truth.
  4. A continuous-time transition prior accounting for irregular inter-visit intervals, roughly six rates per finding under a birth–death restriction.
  5. Exact finite-state inference producing one trajectory posterior from which TEL, ICR and GTS are derived, giving architectural cross-task consistency.
  6. A systematic evaluation of local-to-global reasoning — comparator calibration, trajectory consistency, and compute/accuracy trade-offs.
Three things this page does not yet establish. The 55–65% target is a hypothesis: it rests on six-way comparator accuracy, which nobody has published — CoCa-CXR’s ~65% is a three-class result. The critical experiment is pairwise-then-greedy composition: if greedy already captures most of the gain, the finding becomes “decomposition matters” rather than “inference matters”, and that paper must be planned for in advance. And the emission model must treat a finding not mentioned in a report as not observed, never as absent — reports are incomplete observations, and collapsing the two would corrupt both labels and inference.
